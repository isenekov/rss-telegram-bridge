# GLR Channel Bridge terms

GLR Channel Bridge is operated independently of Reddit and Telegram. It sends
titles and original links for new public r/GamingLeaksAndRumours posts to the
operator's GLAR Telegram channel when the installation's delivery settings are
enabled. It runs in the operator's private Devvit test subreddit.

The operator uses the app in accordance with the permissions granted by Reddit
and applicable content rights. Links lead to the original discussion. If the
app detects that an original has become unavailable, it preserves the delivered
Telegram message's title and link. The operator handles content removal requests
manually and can pause new posts in the installation settings.

Delivery and source checks depend on Devvit, Reddit and Telegram availability.
Posts can arrive late, be skipped when unavailable, or need operator attention
after an unconfirmed Telegram response.

For details on stored data, see the [Privacy notice](PRIVACY.md).
Send support and removal requests to the operator through
[r/gfar34_dev modmail](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=r%2Fgfar34_dev).
